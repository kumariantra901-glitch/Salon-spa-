Our Story

Built as the spa our founder couldn't find.

Lumière opened in 2014 after our founder, a former physical therapist, grew frustrated with day spas that treated relaxation as an upsell. She built a practice around ten private rooms, licensed therapists only, and appointments that are never double-booked.

Twelve years later, that's still the whole idea. We haven't added a juice bar or a retail wall. We added more massage rooms instead.

— Founder, Lumière Day Spa

Before You Visit

A few things people ask.

What should I do before my first visit?

Arrive 15 minutes early to fill out a brief intake form and change. We'll ask about any injuries, allergies, or pressure preferences before your treatment begins.

What's your cancellation policy?

Reschedule or cancel up to 12 hours before your appointment with no fee. Cancellations within 12 hours are charged 50% of the service price.

Do you accept walk-ins?

We accommodate walk-ins when a room is open, but booking ahead guarantees your preferred time and therapist.

What product lines do you use?

We use botanical, cruelty-free lines across every treatment room, and can accommodate sensitive-skin requests — just let us know when booking.
